HOW IT WORKS

From upload to proof in four steps

CertiFlex anchors every certificate on-chain so issuers, holders, and verifiers share one source of truth.

01

Connect Wallet

Link MetaMask to Polygon Amoy and sign in as an authorized exporter or verifier.

02

Upload Certificate

Submit the PDF or document. A unique hash is generated and sent to the smart contract.

03

Stored On-Chain

The hash is immutably recorded on the blockchain—tamper-proof and publicly auditable.

04

Verify Instantly

Anyone can upload the same file and confirm it matches the on-chain record in seconds.
